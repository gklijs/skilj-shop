Seller & finance walkthrough

Follow the terminal walkthrough in the README to query the same purchase as a seller and as finance.

Seller fulfillment

The seller gets the order reference, product description, quantity, and shipping address for their own orders.

Finance reconciliation

Finance gets the order reference, customer email, price paid, and currency across all sellers’ orders.

Access boundaries

GraphQL denies requests for another role’s view or, for sellers, another seller’s order.
